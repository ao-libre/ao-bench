# Cities, Dungeons, and Notable Zones

Sourced from the ao-libre community wiki (v0.13.x).

---

## Overview

Cities are **safe zones** — no player-vs-player combat is possible inside them,
regardless of seguro state. They contain shops (weapons, armor, food, potions),
banks, churches (resurrection + healing), and houses.

Characters spawn at Nemahuak (the newbie city) for levels 1–12, then move
freely across the world.

## Cities

| City | Description | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Nemahuak** | Newbie city. Full facilities for early levels. Has portals to other cities. Class-specific buildings with trainer NPCs. North: magic training. East: melee. West: ranged. South: unsafe zone. | Only accessible to Newbies (level 1–12). No faction alignment required. |
| **Banderbill** | Large city. Casa Central Banco Goliath, Palacio Real, Gran Consejo. Large dock to the north for boat travel. Many shops, farms, and a prison. Has the Coliseo arena for training with summoned creatures. | Main starting city after newbie phase. Map 1. |
| **Nix** | Popular city, popular with beginners. Has a small dock for fishing access. Bank, church, can sail to open sea. | Good for Pesca skill. |
| **Ullathorpe** | Most visited city, located at the center of the world. Good number of shops and houses, bank and church. | Central hub for trading. |
| **Lindos** | Island city, no Imperial Guards — attractive for Criminals. Small village, few shops. Has giant non-hostile turtles useful for training. Bank and church are in the residential area away from shops. | Safe for Criminals. |
| **Esperanza** | Island city, purely commercial — no private houses. Exotic clothing shops, multiple docks. South of the island has hostile creatures. | Tourist/commercial stop. |
| **Arkhêin** | Under demon control. Only city with a theatre ("El Juglar"). Place to enlist in the Legión Oscura. | Legión Oscura headquarters. |
| **Arghâl** | Former demon fortress, now ruins rebuilt by the Ejército Real. Open to all Newbies. | Second newbie-accessible city. |
| **Ciudad del Totem** | Home of the Dwarves. Access only through underground catacombs. Map 279. | Requires navigation through dungeons. |
| **Morgolock's Isle** | Peaceful island with powerful Imperial Druids. Resting place of Gulfas Morgolock. Accessible only by boat. | No combat, lore location. |

## Dungeons and Notable Zones

| Zone | Description | Recommended for |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Dungeon Dragón** (Map 166) | Lair of the Gran Dragón Rojo. Ogros and Beholders. Kill the red dragon to get its mount drop. | Magic classes |
| **Dungeon Marabel** | Hada Oscura, Demonio, Liche, arañas, duendes. | Advanced players |
| **Dungeon Veriil** | Very dangerous. Magical creatures. Accessible from a remote island. | Very advanced |
| **Dungeon Cementerio** | Destroyed cemetery leading to a central Mausoleum. Undead creatures. | Semi-magic classes, mid-level |
| **Dungeon Infierno** | Unusual zone. Better for gold drops than XP. | Gold farming |
| **Montaña Ungdrin Ankor** (Map 270) | Magic doesn't work here. Spiders, zombies, deep dwarves. Gran Dragón Dorado (drops gold dragon mount). | Non-magic classes |
| **Fuerte Pretoriano** | Hidden entrance via dark labyrinth. Intelligent cooperative creatures defending the Pretoriano King. High-level only. | Elite players |
| **Isla Pirata & Ruinas de Ëarost** | Pirates and ghost galleons guard cavern entrance. Deep: Kraken and Leviatan. Magic doesn't work in the deepest part. | Non-magic classes (caves) |
| **Beleta** | Small island with a dock and some creatures. | Magic classes |
| **Bosque élfico** | Ancient elven forest with giant trees, strange creatures, and rare raw materials. | Gathering (dangerous) |
| **Dungeon Tierra del Totem** (Map 199) | Etognils that drop gold/silver/bronze ingots. Gran Dragón Negro (drops black dragon mount). Access via Ciudad del Totem. | Mining/advanced |
| **Ducto de Escape** | Hidden entrance, used as an escape route from the Northern Kingdom Castle. | Lore area |

## Practical implications for agents

- **Map 1 (Banderbill)** is the aobench default starting map. Beginner NPCs (Gallo, Rata, Cerdo, Conejo) spawn around x=40–60, y=40–60.
- **Cities are safe zones** — no XP from combat inside them. Go outside city boundaries to fight.
- **Dungeons** offer higher XP per kill but also higher danger. Dungeon maps vary — some disable magic (Montaña Ungdrin Ankor, Isla Pirata deep caves).
- **Map transitions** give 5 seconds of immunity from hostile creatures. Don't attack, move, or speak to preserve it.
- **Nemahuak portals** provide fast travel between cities for newbie characters (levels 1–12 only).
- **Mining locations** matter for Minería tasks — the nearest accessible mine is in the catacombs east of Nix.
